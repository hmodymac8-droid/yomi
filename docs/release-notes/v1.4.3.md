### What's new
- **Smoother scrolling.** Cards no longer lift, zoom or start loading as they pass under the mouse while you scroll, and title cards no longer grow as they come into view.
- **A calmer Discover.** Continue reading comes first, one card per series. New chapters is a slim line of small chips under it. Because you read is one row.
- **Shorter site lists.** Title cards show four sites, then "+ N more". Hover to see them all.
- **Similar on your sites folds.** It shows 6 titles, with Show all for the rest, and remembers whether it is open.
- **The series cover keeps its size** instead of stretching down the page.

### Removed
- **Extensions.** The optional extension system and its Settings section are gone, so nothing extra starts with the app. Sites you already added keep working.

- **تمرير أنعم.** وأنت تمرّر، الكروت اللي تمر تحت الماوس ما عادت ترتفع أو تكبّر غلافها أو تبدأ تحمّل العمل. وكروت العناوين ما عادت تكبر لما توصل لها.
