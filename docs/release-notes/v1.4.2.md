### What's new
- **Mini reader.** While you are reading, the picture-in-picture button opens a small window that stays on top of other apps. Drag it, pin or unpin it, and keep scrolling. Expand brings the chapter back into the main window. Closing the mini reader does not close Yomi, and your library stays where it was.
