### What's new
- **Far fewer freezes.** The app no longer stops for seconds at a time after it opens. The longest pause at startup went from about 5.6 s to under half a second.
- **Search keeps the app responsive.** The first search after opening (All titles, filters, sorting) is now worked out in small pieces, so covers keep loading and clicks still answer while it builds.
- **Series pages open instantly.** "Similar on your sites" and "Because you read" no longer search the whole catalog once per title. Opening a series dropped from almost 2 s to a few hundredths of a second.
- **Quieter background work.** Saving the catalog and tidying old entries now wait until you stop moving the mouse or using the keyboard, so they never land in the middle of a scroll or a page turn.
